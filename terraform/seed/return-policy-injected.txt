RETURN & REFUND POLICY

Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided they are unused and in original packaging. Refunds are issued
to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of us receiving
the returned item.

Damaged or defective items may be returned at any time within 90 days.

For questions about a specific order, contact support with your order ID.
